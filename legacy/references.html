What we do on site

Engineering and design

  • DCS conceptual architecture, I/O databases, control logic and control narratives, HMI design and alarm schemes
  • ESD and Burner Management System architecture, test and FAT procedures
  • SIL 2 and SIL 3 certification support to IEC 61511
  • I&C design verification against local and international standards
  • P&ID, cable schedules, panel design, hook-up and as-built documentation
  • Procurement of control and instrumentation equipment

Commissioning and operation

  • Factory and site acceptance testing, loop checks, functional testing, loop tuning
  • Autonomous plant operation with remote control over four redundant IEC 60870-5-104 channels
  • Turbine acceptance testing: quality of primary and secondary frequency regulation, rate of load change, generator disconnection from the grid, emergency stops
  • Boiler efficiency by direct and indirect method for gas and petroleum coke fired CFB boilers; steam turbine efficiency and losses; thermal and electrical energy balances
  • Process data collection from control systems and commercial metering, long-term archiving and calculation
  • DCS backup, lifecycle updates, and protection of control system assets against internal and external cyber threats
  • Operator training on new control schemes

Featured projects

Advanced load control, CFB boiler

Energy boiler, 260 t/h steam at 100 bar, circulating fluidized bed, firing petroleum coke, coal, gas and fuel oil, on DCS Emerson Ovation.

Delivered: coordinated boiler–turbine load control; solid fuel allocation across feeders by heat value; steam temperature control by cascade regulators with a superheater model; primary and secondary air control; SO2 and NOx regulation through furnace temperature and lime dosing; automatic calculation of petroleum coke calorific value; and an oxygen cascade controller referenced to boiler mode and fuel.

Read more

Virtual power plant

Four photovoltaic plants, one wind farm and one conventional gas/coal plant with boilers and steam turbines combined into a single dispatch unit: three remote PV control systems at 1 MW each, a local PV control system at 9 MW and a remote wind control system at 80 MW, all connected to the gas/coal plant control system, with output balanced by the steam turbine to a predetermined schedule.

Read more

Plant performance monitoring

Real-time efficiency of boilers, turbines and heat exchangers against design values, with the cost of losses and deviations from factory specification calculated continuously, plus energy and material balances for water, steam, gas and electricity. Data from DCS ABB Industrial IT and DCS Emerson Ovation over OPC, and from SCADA and commercial metering over Modbus, with central long-term archiving. Calculations follow ASME and other industry standards.
