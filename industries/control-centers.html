Control Centers

Control Center Design for Top Industry Leaders

ISYSTEMS AUTOMATION has achieved a significant milestone in the design, manufacture, and supply of cutting-edge equipment for several command centers. Our esteemed clients include industry giants such as LUKOIL Energy and Gas, Dalkia Termo Prahova, NuclearElectrica, along with numerous smaller companies.

The new control centers are designed with the latest technological advancements, ensuring that they meet the highest standards of safety and reliability. The furniture, meticulously crafted, complements these designs by providing ergonomic solutions that cater to the unique needs of control room operators.

"Our designs are not only about enhancing the operational efficiency but also about improving the overall well-being of the operators who spend long hours in these environments."

Key features of the new offering include:

- Ergonomic Furniture: Designed to reduce fatigue and improve productivity, the furniture includes adjustable desks, comfortable seating, and smart storage solutions.

- Advanced Control Centers: Equipped with cutting-edge technology, these centers ensure seamless monitoring and management of industrial operations.

- Customizable Solutions: Both the control centers and the furniture can be tailored to meet specific industry requirements, offering unparalleled flexibility.
