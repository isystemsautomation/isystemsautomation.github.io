Power Engineering:
Fundamental Electrical Engineering Excellence

Since our establishment, our company has been at the forefront of automating the production processes of electricity and heat. Our specialists possess in-depth knowledge and extensive experience in these critical areas, ensuring top-notch expertise and innovation in power engineering.

Areas of Expertise of Our Specialists:

- Condensed Steam Turbine: Expertly designed for efficient energy conversion from steam.

- Back Pressure Steam Turbine: Specialized in optimizing energy recovery in industrial processes.

- Gas Turbines: Advanced solutions for high-efficiency power generation.

- Hydro Turbine: Cutting-edge technology for sustainable hydroelectric power.

- Heat Recovery Steam Generator: Maximizing energy efficiency by recovering waste heat.

- Steam Boilers with Gas Combustion Process: Precision-engineered for cleaner, more efficient combustion.

- Steam Boilers with Liquid Fuel Burning Process: Optimized for diverse fuel types and efficient operation.

- Steam Boilers with Mixed Burning Process: Innovative fluidized bed technology for versatile fuel usage (gas/solid fuel/semi-liquid fuel).

- AAF Bags for Cleaning Ash: Effective ash removal systems for steam boilers burning solid fuel.

- City Heating System - District Heating: Comprehensive solutions for efficient and reliable urban heating.

- Eolian Powerplant: Harnessing wind energy for sustainable power generation.

Our commitment to excellence in power engineering and fundamental electrical engineering is unwavering. We continue to push the boundaries of innovation, ensuring that our clients receive the highest quality solutions for their power generation needs.
